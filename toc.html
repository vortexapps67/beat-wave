1. Data Collection

BeatWave is built with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal music files. All audio processing happens locally on your device, ensuring your listening habits remain yours alone.

2. Permissions

The application requires minimal access to your device's storage strictly to scan and play your local audio files. These permissions are siloed and used exclusively for playback functionality.

3. Open Source Foundation

As an open-source project, our logic is entirely transparent. You are free to inspect the source code on our official GitHub repository to verify our security standards and community-driven updates.
